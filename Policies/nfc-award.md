# Standing Knowledge — National Finance Commission (NFC) Award

Contributed by: EP004 ("The NFC Award: The Formula That Divides Pakistan's Money")

## Summary

Article 160 of the 1973 Constitution requires the President to periodically constitute a National Finance Commission to decide how federal tax revenue (the "divisible pool") splits between the federation and the four provinces, and among the provinces themselves. From 1973–2009, population alone decided each province's share. The **7th NFC Award (2009)** introduced a multi-criteria formula — Population 82%, Poverty/Backwardness 10.3%, Revenue Collection/Generation 5%, Area 2.7% — and raised the provinces' total share of the divisible pool to 57.5%. That formula's five-year term expired in **2015**, but no successor has been agreed since; it has continued to apply by default for over a decade (exact legal mechanism for this continuation not yet confirmed — see EP004's Open Questions).

## ⚠ Live process, stalled (as of Aug 2026)

The **11th NFC** was constituted 22 August 2025, chaired by Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb. Its first actual meeting wasn't held until 4 December 2025. Eight technical working groups were formed but only 2 had met even once by the most recent reporting found (~Jan-Feb 2026); the process has been repeatedly delayed, with federal and provincial sides blaming each other. **Re-verify current status before any future episode treats this as resolved.**

## Key figures

- Current divisible pool split: 42.5% federal / 57.5% provincial
- 7th Award formula: Population 82%, Poverty/Backwardness 10.3%, Revenue Collection/Generation 5%, Area 2.7%
- 18th Amendment's Article 160(3A): no province's NFC share can be reduced below its previous award — a structural "ratchet" protecting provinces in any future negotiation

## Provincial positions (sourced, see EP004 for detail)

- **Sindh:** wanted (and got, in the 7th Award) revenue collection/generation counted — benefits from being the main port/largest tax-collection base
- **KP:** wanted poverty/backwardness weighted; also carries the specific fiscal burden of the former FATA's 2018 merger
- **Balochistan:** wants population's weight reduced and area's weight increased (large land area, small population)
- **Punjab:** not yet sourced directly; structurally favored by the current population-heavy weighting given it's the most populous province
