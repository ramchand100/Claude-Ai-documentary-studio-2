## Notes

**Spine (from the outline):** A working train that once carried 6 million Karachiites a year has been "about to come back" for over two decades, under four governments and three different foreign financiers — and the same structural standoff has stopped every single attempt.

**Estimated runtime:** 1,433 words ÷ 140wpm ≈ 10.2 minutes raw narration, plus ~20% for pacing/breathing room ≈ **~12.3 minutes**. Lands right at the low end of the outline's 12-16 minute range — no padding needed to hit it.

**Structural note carried from the outline:** this script deliberately does NOT narrate all 14+ dated revival attempts found in the research. Only the 2005 Shaukat Aziz attempt and two full "cycles" (JICA, then CPEC) are spoken in full — the remaining dated promises (2010 Bilour, 2012 Khilji, the 2017-18 slipped start dates, etc.) are left for Visual Planning's on-screen text/timeline graphic. This is a deliberate compression, not an oversight — narrating all 14 would work against the point of this structure (the pattern, not the tally, is the story).

**Editorial flags:**
- **The illustrative commuter** ("socho ek aam Karachi ka mulazim...") introduced in Section 2 and called back in Section 6 is explicitly NOT a real, sourced person — the research found no sourced individual rider or displaced-resident account. Flagged inline in the narration itself ("yeh koi haqiqi shakhs nahi, sirf ek misaal hai") per the illustrative-analogy rule, since no real example existed in the research.
- **Displaced residents' own perspective is deliberately absent** — the research brief flagged this as an unsourced gap (same handling as EP004's unsourced Punjab position). Section 4 states the anti-encroachment operation factually (scale, that a review petition was filed) without speaking for the affected families' viewpoint.
- **The Dec 2018 displacement figure is scoped carefully**: ~1,200 encroachments were KCR-specific, but the ~4,653 families figure is the city-wide railway-land total from the same operation, not KCR-specific alone. The script states both figures with their correct scope rather than conflating them — this distinction came directly from a flag in the research brief.
- **Founding year stated as 1964** without hedging on-screen, per the outline's guidance to use the better-corroborated figure rather than presenting the 1962/1969-70 outlier as equally likely.
- **JICA's exact financing terms (0.1% vs 0.2% interest) are deliberately not stated as a precise number** — the script describes the financing qualitatively ("bohot kam byaaj, lambi muddat" — very low interest, long term) rather than committing to either disputed figure, per the outline's guidance.
- **Section 4 is intentionally the longest section** — it compresses the entire CPEC-era cycle (2016-2026), which is where most of the dated material lives. Numbers are deliberately kept one-per-paragraph with a break between each, rather than stacked, to avoid the zero-background-listener overload the "100-rupee" rule and the zero-background check both warn against — but it's worth your read to confirm the pacing still feels right, since it covers more ground than any other section in the episode.
- **The "kooRa" (garbage train) anecdote from the research brief was left out entirely** — it's Tier 4/5-sourced only and not essential to the spine, so it was cut rather than included with a hedge.
- **The CDWP→ECNEC cost jump (Rs27.6bn to ~Rs207bn within one year) is stated as "roughly sevenfold"** — this is a real, sourced escalation between two different approval stages of the same project, not a sourcing contradiction, per the research brief's explicit flag.

## Hook / Cold Open

May 2026 mein, Asian Development Bank ne Karachi ke liye ek billion dollar dene ka wada kiya — taake shehar ki ek purani train wapas chalayi ja sake.

Yeh sunne mein bohot bara elaan lagta hai. Lekin agar aap Karachi mein purane hain, toh shayad yeh jumla aap pehle bhi sun chuke hon.

Kyunke pichle teen dahaiyon mein, yeh wada — "train wapas aayegi" — kam se kam chaudah baar kiya ja chuka hai. Aur har baar, kuch aisa hi hua: elaan hua, phir koi masla aaya, phir sab ruk gaya.

Toh sawaal yeh nahi ke wada kya hai. Sawaal yeh hai — kya is baar waqai kuch farak hai?

## Section 1 — What Karachi Actually Lost

Is sawaal ka jawab samajhne ke liye, pehle yeh samajhna zaroori hai ke Karachi ne asal mein khoya kya tha.

1964 mein, Karachi ko mili ek circular train — yaani ek aisi train jo poore shehar ke gird ek chakkar lagati thi, City Station se shuru hokar wapas usi jagah aati thi. Iska raasta takreeban 43 kilometer lamba tha, aur is par 24 stations thay.

Apne zamane mein, yeh train din mein 104 baar chalti thi, aur saal mein takreeban 60 lakh log isay istemal karte thay.

Socho ek aam Karachi ka mulazim — yeh koi haqiqi shakhs nahi, sirf ek misaal hai — jo Landhi mein rehta ho aur City Station ke qareeb kaam karta ho. Us waqt, woh subah train mein baithta, aur bina kisi traffic jam ke, seedha apne kaam par pahunch jata.

Lekin 1990s mein, cheezein badalne lagien. Train ka kharcha barhta gaya, kiraya kam raha, aur maintenance kamzor hoti gayi. Private buses aur vans ka network barhne laga — aur reported hai ke kuch railway staff aur road transport walon ke darmiyan bhi saazbaaz thi, jisne train ki sawari aur bhi kam kar di.

December 1999 tak, Pakistan Railways ne yeh train mukammal taur par band kar di. Wajah bataayi gayi: ab yeh chalana munafa bakhsh nahi raha.

Us din ke baad se, is train ko wapas laane ka wada, baar baar kiya gaya hai. Aur har baar, ek jaisa cycle dohraya gaya hai.

## Section 2 — The Cycle

2005 mein, Prime Minister Shaukat Aziz ne is train ke ek hisse ko dobara shuru kiya, aur 3.5 arab rupaye dene ka wada kiya. Lekin saal bhar ke andar, yeh hissa bhi band ho gaya.

Phir 2009 mein, ek zyada sanjeeda koshish shuru hui — is baar Japan ki ek sarkari sanstha, JICA, ke saath. JICA ne poori train ki bahali ka mutalya kiya, aur Pakistan ki planning committee, ECNEC, ne is mansooba ko manzoor kar liya.

Lekin jaise jaise mutalya aagay barha, ek purana masla saamne aaya: is train ke raaste par, hazaron ghar aur dukanein ban chuki thin — log jo dahaiyon se wahan reh rahe thay. JICA ke apne sarve ke mutabiq, char hazar se zyada ghar aur chhay sau se zyada dukanein is zameen par thin.

Aur yahan ek aisa masla khada hua jo aage bhi baar baar aayega: JICA ka kehna tha ke pehle yeh log hataye jayein, phir woh paisa dega. Sindh hukumat ka kehna tha ke pehle paisa pakka ho, phir woh logon ko hatayegi.

Koi bhi pehle qadam uthane ko tayyar nahi tha. Aur 2016 mein, JICA ne yeh mansooba chhod diya.

Lekin yeh kahani yahan khatam nahi hui. Kyunke jis hafte JICA ne haath khencha, Sindh hukumat ne turant ek naya rasta dhoond liya.

## Section 3 — The Cycle Repeats

Sindh ke Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah ne foran cheen se rabta kiya. Aur usi mahine, is train ko CPEC — yaani China-Pakistan Economic Corridor — mein shamil kar liya gaya.

2017 ki shuruat mein, ek sarkari committee ne is mansooba ki lagat 27 arab rupaye lagayi. Lekin sirf kuch mahinon baad, jab is par aakhri manzoori mili, toh yehi lagat barh kar takreeban 207 arab rupaye ho chuki thi — yaani ek hi saal mein, andaza roughly saat guna barh gaya.

October 2017 mein, is naye mansoobe ko manzoori mil gayi. Cheen is ka bara hissa fund karne wala tha. Kaam 2020 tak mukammal hone ka wada kiya gaya.

Lekin ek baar phir, wahi purana masla — zameen par ban chuke ghar aur dukanein — raaste mein aa gaya.

November 2018 mein, Supreme Court ne khud is maamle mein dakhal diya. Adalat ne hukum diya ke train ke raaste se naajaiz tameerat hatayi jayein.

Ek mahine baad, ek operation shuru hua. KCR ke apne raaste se takreeban 1,200 illegal tameerat hatayi gayin — aur is dauran, shehar bhar ki railway zameen se, kul milakar saade char hazar khandaan mutasir huay.

Yeh koi aasan faisla nahi tha, aur iske khilaf ek petition bhi dayer hui.

Lekin operation ke baad, kaam thoda aagay barha. 2020 mein, hukumat ne saade das arab rupaye ka ek chhota package manzoor kiya — mukammal mansooba nahi, balke sirf ek hisse ki bahali ke liye.

Aur November 2020 mein, kuch aisa hua jo 1999 ke baad pehli baar hua tha: City Station se Orangi tak, 14 kilometer ka ek hissa, dobara chalna shuru hua. Din mein sirf chaar train — lekin yeh 21 saal mein pehli baar tha ke koi train is route par waqai chal rahi thi.

Aur yahan ek dilchasp baat hai: yehi 14 kilometer, aaj tak, is poori kahani ka wahid hissa hai jo waqai zameen par utra hai.

Is chhoti si kaamyabi ke baad, ek aur bara elaan hua. September 2021 mein, poore mansoobe ki bunyad rakhi gayi — is baar takreeban 207 arab rupaye ki lagat se, teen saal ke andar mukammal hone ka wada.

Lekin agle saal, lagat ek baar phir badli — pehle 270 arab, phir 201 arab rupaye tak wapas layi gayi. 2022 mein hi, dobara wada kiya gaya ke yeh kaam 2025 tak mukammal ho jayega.

2023 mein ek aur koshish hui — Sindh hukumat ne cheen ki ek company ke saath dobara baat cheet ki, zameen ka masla hal karne ke liye. Lekin is baar bhi, zameen par koi naya kaam shuru nahi hua.

2025 aa kar guzar gaya. Poori 43 kilometer ki train, ab bhi nahi chal rahi.

Aur Dawn akhbar ki apni reporting ke mutabiq, February 2026 tak, yeh mansooba CPEC ke andar "ek pura dahaka bina kisi pesh raft ke" guzar chuka tha.

## Section 4 — Back to the Present

Aur yehi woh mor hai jahan hum wapas us billion dollar wale elaan tak aate hain, jahan se humne yeh kahani shuru ki thi.

May 2026 mein, jab Asian Development Bank ne yeh billion dollar ka wada kiya, toh yeh sach mein ek nayi, bari raqam thi — is se pehle na JICA, na Cheen, itni bari raqam ka wada kar chuka tha.

Lekin ek cheez ghor karne wali hai: is poore billion dollar mein se, abhi sirf das million dollar foran diya gaya hai — yani wada ki hui raqam ka bohot chhota sa hissa. Aur woh bhi tameer ke liye nahi, balke sirf planning ke liye hai: design dobara dekhna, operation ka tareeqa tay karna, ada'ra bandi karna.

Yani abhi tak, yeh mansooba usi marhale par hai jahan pichle teen koshishein bhi thin — wada ho chuka hai, tameer shuru nahi hui.

Kya is baar farak hoga, yeh kehna abhi mushkil hai. Lekin jo cheez badalni hogi, woh wahi hai jo har baar rukawat bani: zameen ka masla hal hona, aur financier aur hukumat, dono ka, pehle qadam uthane se na darna.

Is sab ke darmiyan, ek sawaal peeche reh jata hai: jab tak yeh train wapas nahi aati, Karachi khud kya jhel raha hai?

---

## Section 5 — Why a Train Nobody's Riding Still Matters

Aaj Karachi mein 2 crore se zyada log rehte hain — aur shehar ka zyada tar safar, sadkon par hota hai. Takreeban 41 lakh gaadiyan is shehar ki sadkon par hain, aur har mahine hazaron aur gaadiyan shamil ho rahi hain.

Is traffic jam ki keemat, ek research ke mutabiq, har saal takreeban 200 arab rupaye hai. Agar Pakistan ki poori mulki paidawar ko 100 rupaye maan liya jaye, toh sirf Karachi ka traffic jam, har saal in mein se takreeban derh rupaya barbad kar deta hai.

Yaad karein woh mulazim jiska zikar humne shuru mein kiya tha — jo Landhi se City Station tak, kabhi train mein baith kar, bina kisi rukawat ke pahunch jata tha. Aaj, waisa hi koi shakhs, roz ghanton traffic mein guzarta hai — kyunke woh option ab maujood nahi.

Yeh train, jab tak wapas nahi aati, sirf ek purani yaad nahi rehti. Yeh har roz, har Karachi wale ke waqt aur paise ki asli keemat hai.
