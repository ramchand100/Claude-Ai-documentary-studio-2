## Section 1 — The Price Tag

## Section 2 — The Tax Stack

**Narration:**

Chaliye is qeemat ke andar dekhte hain.

Sabse pehli tehe hai customs duty — yeh woh tax hai jo koi bhi cheez mulk mein import hone par lagta hai. Gaadiyon ke liye yeh tax bohot zyada hai, khaas taur par un cars par jo poori tarah bahar se import hoti hain — inhein "Completely Built Unit," ya CBU, kehte hain.

Uske upar hai regulatory duty — ek aur tax jo sarkar specific cheezon par extra laga sakti hai, mulk ke industries ko protect karne ke liye. Ek recent regulation ke tehet, CBU imports par yeh duty 10 se 50 percent tak lagti hai.

Phir aata hai sales tax — yeh woh tax hai jo har khareedari par lagta hai, chahe car locally hi kyun na bani ho. March 2023 mein, locally assembled cars par yeh sales tax barh kar 25 percent ho gaya.

Aur akhri mein hai federal excise duty — engine ke size ke hisab se ek aur charge.

Yeh sab milakar ek simple si baat batate hain: jab aap ek car ki qeemat dekhte hain, aap sirf car nahi khareed rahe — aap in sab taxes ka majmua bhi khareed rahe hain.

## Section 3 — Why It Was Built This Way

**Narration:**

Yeh sab taxes achanak nahi aaye. Yeh ek soch samjhi policy ka nateeja hain jo dahaiyon purani hai.

1980s ke shuru mein, Pakistan mein car banane ki industry bilkul nayi thi. Sarkar ko dar tha ke agar imports par restriction na lagayi gayi, toh bahar ki badi companies apni poori bani banayi cars bhej kar Pakistan ki apni industry ko kabhi bhi khada hi nahi hone dengi.

Toh 1987 se 2004 tak, ek policy chali jise "Deletion Program" kehte hain — har car maker ke liye zaroori tha ke woh apni car ke parts ka ek barhta hua hissa Pakistan ke andar hi banwaye. Aur yeh kaam kiya — cars mein 56 se 70 percent tak, aur motorcycles mein 81 se 88 percent tak, local parts istemaal hone lage.

Lekin ek limit thi: engine jaisi advanced, high-value cheezein ab bhi bahar se hi aati rahin.

2006 mein yeh policy badal kar "Tariff-Based System" ban gayi — ab local parts banana zaroori nahi tha, lekin agar koi poori car import karta, toh us par duty engine ke size ke hisab se 50 se 100 percent, kabhi kabhi usse bhi zyada lag sakti thi.

Matlab seedhi si baat: local banao, sasta rahega. Bahar se lao, mehnga hoga. Yehi soch aaj tak in taxes ke peeche khadi hai.

## Section 4 — Did It Work?

**Narration:**

Toh sawaal yeh hai: kya yeh protection kaam aayi?

Jawaab mushkil hai — kyunke dono baatein sach hain.

Haan, ek local industry banii. Aaj Pakistan mein 13 alag alag car companies gaadiyan bana rahi hain. Lekin market abhi bhi teen companies ke haath mein hai — Suzuki, Toyota, aur Honda, jinhein log "Big Three" kehte hain. In teenon ka combined market share hai takreeban 79 se 80 percent.

Pakistan ke apne competition regulator — Competition Commission of Pakistan — ne is market ko officially "highly concentrated" declare kiya hai, yaani itni kam companies ka itna zyada control ke healthy competition mushkil ho jaati hai.

Aur is concentration ka nateeja sirf theory nahi hai. 2013 mein, Competition Commission ne Toyota Pakistan ko notice diya — ilzam yeh tha ke unke booking order ke terms customers ke liye unfair the, delivery schedule par unilateral control tha, bina pehle bataye.

2015 mein, ek automotive dealers association ko price-fixing ka qasoorwar paaya gaya — repair aur paint ke rates sab jagah ek jaisay rakhe ja rahe the, competition khatam karne ke liye. Commission ne 14 crore rupees ka jurmana lagaya.

Toh haan — protection ne ek industry banayi. Lekin usi protection ne aisi market bhi banayi jahan kabhi kabhi bade players apni position ka galat istemaal karte rahe.
