## Hook / Cold Open

**Narration:**

Zara socho. Aap ne apni mehnat ki kamai dollars mein bachai hui hai — apne bank account mein, legally, sarkar ki hi encourage ki hui scheme ke tehat. Aur ek subah aap bank jaate hain, apne hi paise nikalne, aur aapko bataya jaata hai: nahi, yeh paisa abhi nahi milega.

Yeh koi kahani nahi hai. Yeh 1998 mein Pakistan mein sach mein hua. Ek hi hafte mein, sarkar ne up to 11 billion dollars — logon ke apne, legal, foreign currency accounts — freeze kar diye. Overnight.

Sawaal yeh nahi ke yeh hua kaise. Sawaal yeh hai: kyun? Aur woh jawaab shuru hota hai do hafte pehle, Balochistan ke ek registan mein.

## Section 1 — The Test

**Narration:**

May 1998. India pehle hi apne nuclear tests kar chuki thi — 11 aur 13 May ko, Pokhran mein. Pakistan ke liye yeh sirf ek headline nahi thi — yeh ek fifty-year-old strategic sawaal tha: agar India ke paas hai, toh hum bhi... nahi karenge?

28 May, 1998. Pakistan ne Balochistan ke Chagai district mein, Ras Koh Hills par, paanch nuclear tests kiye — codename Chagai-I. Do din baad, 30 May ko, ek aur test hua — Chagai-II.

Poore mulk mein jashan tha. Sadkon par mithai baant di gayi. Yeh national pride ka moment tha.

Lekin jashan ke peeche, ek doosri kahani shuru ho rahi thi — aur woh kahani economy ki thi.

## Section 2 — The Bill Comes Due

**Narration:**

Yahan se cheezein tezi se badalti hain.

Pakistan us waqt already IMF ke saath ek three-year program mein tha — October 1997 se — jiska maqsad tha current account deficit kam karna aur reserves banana. Aur 1998 ke shuru mein, IMF ne khud kaha tha ke Pakistan theek chal raha hai. Program continue hua.

Phir tests hue. Aur duniya ne react kiya.

United States ne, apne Glenn Amendment ke tehat, foran Pakistan par economic sanctions laga diye. Japan ne bhi apni aid rok di. Yeh koi symbolic gesture nahi tha — yeh un mulkon ka access tha jo Pakistan ki economy ko chalne mein madad de rahe the.

Ek fragile, IMF-supported program — jo mahine pehle "on track" tha — ab apne aap tootne laga.

## Section 3 — The Freeze

**Narration:**

Ab sarkar ke saamne ek problem thi jo roz badh rahi thi: log dar gaye the. Jab logon ko lagta hai ke mulk ka paisa khatam ho raha hai, toh sab ek hi kaam karte hain — apna paisa nikaal lo, jitni jaldi ho sake.

Yeh bank run kehlaata hai. Aur agar yeh rukta nahi, toh reserves — jo already gir rahe the — mukammal khatam ho sakte the.

Toh sarkar ne ek extraordinary faisla liya. Har foreign currency account — private aur government banks dono mein — freeze kar diya gaya. Withdrawal band. Finance Minister Sartaj Aziz ne khud iski wajah batayi: capital flight ko rokna tha, warna mulk bankruptcy ke qareeb pahunch sakta tha.

Lekin yahan ek irony hai jo shayad aap na jaante hon: yeh wahi log the jinhein pehle encourage kiya gaya tha apna dollar Pakistan ke andar hi bank karne — tax incentives ke saath, disclosure requirements ke saath. Woh trust hi tha jisse sarkar ne kaam liya. Aur ab wahi trust tod diya gaya.

Kitna paisa? Andaazan up to 11 billion dollars.

## Section 4 — The Countdown

**Narration:**

Ab sawaal yeh hai: sarkar itni ghabrayi kyun thi?

Kyunke reserves waqai khatam ho rahe the. November 1998 tak, Pakistan ke official foreign exchange reserves gir kar sirf takreeban 400 million dollars reh gaye the. Yeh sunne mein bhi bara lagta hai — lekin ek mulk ke liye jo imports par chalta hai, yeh sirf chand hafton ka kharcha hai.

Aur is dauraan currency par bhi pressure tha. Official rate 46 rupees per dollar tha — lekin agar aap black market mein jaate, toh rate 54 rupees tha. Yeh gap khud bata raha tha ke system ke andar kitna real pressure tha, jitna official numbers dikhate the.

Yeh woh moment tha jab Pakistan formal default se sirf kuch hafton door tha.

**[VISUAL NOTE: Animated reserves counter dropping toward $400M — visualize as "weeks of import cover remaining" ticking down, not just a raw dollar figure. Side-by-side comparison graphic: official rate (46) vs. parallel market rate (54).]**

## Section 5 — The Rescue

**Narration:**

14 January, 1999. IMF ne Pakistan ke ESAF program ka doosra saal approve kar diya — takreeban 575 million dollars release hue.

Kuch hafton baad, Paris Club — jo mulkon ka ek group hai jinhein Pakistan qarza deta hai — ne Pakistan ka debt reschedule kar diya. London Club ne bhi aisa hi kiya.

Yeh koi clean victory nahi thi. Pakistan default se bach gaya — lekin naye conditions ke saath, aur zyada qarze ke saath. Jo bill 1998 mein na chuka, woh aage ke saalon mein chukana tha.

## Section 6 — What It Left Behind

**Narration:**

Ab, saalon baad, agar aap kisi purani generation ke Pakistani se poochein ke woh apna paisa dollar mein kahan rakhte hain — bank mein, ya kahin aur — toh bahut logon ka jawaab wahi hoga jo 1998 ne sikhaya: bank mein rakho, aur risk lo ke ek din woh tumhara nahi rahega.

Yeh sirf ek economic crisis nahi tha. Yeh ek trust crisis tha — aur uska asar aaj bhi mehsoos hota hai.

Aur yeh bhi yaad rakhna: 1998 Pakistan ka pehla ya aakhri IMF program nahi tha. Yeh pattern — crisis, rescue, naye conditions — dobara hoga. Kyun? Woh ek doosri kahani hai.
