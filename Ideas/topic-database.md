## Scoring legend (1–5 each, see Topic Research skill for rubric)

- **Curiosity** — does the title alone make someone want to click?

- **Evergreen Value** — will this still matter in 2 years, or is it news-cycle disposable?
- **Storytelling Potential** — is there a narrative arc, or just facts?
- **Research Availability** — can this actually be sourced to Tier 1–3 sources?
