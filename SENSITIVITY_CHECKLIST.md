# Pre-Publish Sensitivity Checklist

Run this manually on every episode before publishing. This is a judgment call — the AI can flag candidates, but you make the final call on what ships.

Referenced by: Fact-Checking skill (flags candidate items), Documentary Pipeline skill (blocks "publish" step until this is checked off).

## 1. Military & security

- [ ] Does the episode reference the armed forces, ISI, or an ongoing security operation?
- [ ] Are claims about military-linked businesses (e.g. Fauji Foundation, DHA, Army Welfare Trust) sourced to public filings/reporting, not speculation?
- [ ] Avoid implying institutional motive or conspiracy without a cited source.

## 2. Religion & sect

- [ ] Does the episode touch a religious institution, sectarian issue, or blasphemy-adjacent topic?
- [ ] Is language neutral and descriptive, not evaluative of any sect or belief?
- [ ] If unavoidable, has the framing been checked against how it could be clipped out of context?

## 3. Ethnicity & province

- [ ] Does the episode discuss Punjab/Sindh/KP/Balochistan/GB/AJK tensions, resource distribution, or nationalist movements?
- [ ] Are all sides' stated positions represented, even if the episode ultimately takes an angle?
- [ ] Avoid language that stereotypes a province or ethnic group.

## 4. Living people & companies

- [ ] Are all factual claims about named individuals or companies sourced (see `SOURCE_TIERS.md`)?
- [ ] Is anything stated as fact actually just allegation or opinion? If so, is it attributed ("according to X")?
- [ ] Could any claim be read as defamatory if untrue? If yes — verify harder or cut it.
- [ ] Ongoing litigation involving a named party — state "as of [date], the matter is before the courts," don't presume outcome.

## 5. Political neutrality

- [ ] Does the episode critique a policy/system rather than attack a party or individual's character?
- [ ] If multiple governments/parties are relevant to the topic's history, are they treated with the same evidentiary standard?
- [ ] No endorsement, implicit or explicit, of any political party.

## 6. Data & numbers

- [ ] Every statistic traceable to a Tier 1–3 source (see `SOURCE_TIERS.md`)?
- [ ] Dated — is this the latest available figure, and is the date stated on screen/in description?

## 7. Legal

- [ ] Any content that could trigger PECA (Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act) concerns — defamation, false information framed as fact?
- [ ] Any copyrighted footage/images used without rights or fair-use justification?

## Outcome

- [ ] Clear to publish as-is
- [ ] Clear to publish with disclaimer (note wording used: ___________)
- [ ] Needs revision before publishing (note what: ___________)
- [ ] Escalate for legal review before publishing
