# Standing Knowledge — Automotive Industry

Contributed by: EP002 ("The Auto Tax: Why a Small Car Costs a House Deposit")

## Summary

Pakistan's car market has historically been dominated by three assemblers — "the Big Three": **Pak Suzuki**, **Indus Motor Company (Toyota)**, and **Honda Atlas Cars**. Their combined market share has been eroding since roughly FY2019 as newer entrants (Hyundai Nishat, KIA Lucky Motors, MG, Changan, and others) enter the market — though the exact current combined share is **contradictory across sources as of this research pass** (one source: ~84% in 2026; another: ~56% in 2026, down from ~80% in 2018) and needs reconciliation before being cited on screen.

## Key figures (flagged confidence noted)

- Suzuki individual share: cited variously as 48.2% (2026), 57% (2019), 60.6% (2019) — inconsistent across sources, treat cautiously
- Suzuki Alto price: ~PKR 2.99-3.3 million (Pakistan, 2025-26) vs ~Rs 3.25-5.12 lakh (India, 2025-26) — commonly cited ~7-8x gap, but see the fairness-caveat open question in EP002's research brief before using this ratio uncritically
