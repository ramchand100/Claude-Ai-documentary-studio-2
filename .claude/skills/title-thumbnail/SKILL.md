# Title and Thumbnail Skill

Generate publish-ready titles and thumbnail concepts.

## Process

1. **Read the finalized script and its hook** — titles should promise exactly what the episode delivers. No mismatch with `CHANNEL_STYLE_GUIDE.md`'s "clickbait the video doesn't deliver on" rule.
2. **Generate 20 titles**, covering a spread of angles, not 20 minor variants of one phrasing:
   - Curiosity-driven (open loop, implied question)
   - Number/stat-driven (a striking figure from the research)
   - Person/company-driven (if the episode centers one)
   - Direct-benefit driven ("why your ___ ...")
   - SEO-friendly, matching how Pakistani viewers actually search (include Roman Urdu search terms where relevant, per `GLOSSARY.md`)
3. **Rank the top 5** with a one-line reason each.
4. **Generate 3-5 thumbnail concepts**: focal image, 3-5 words of on-screen text max, and a note on why it'll read clearly at small mobile size.
5. **Generate hook variations** — 2-3 alternate ways to deliver the same first-30-seconds hook already in the script, in case the editor wants options for the cold open.

## Output

Titles and thumbnail concepts saved to `/Published Videos/EP###_slug-name/thumbnail-brief.md` using `Templates/thumbnail-brief.md` (add a titles section, or keep a companion `titles.md` in the same folder).

## Notes

- Optimize for Pakistani viewer search/scroll behavior specifically — bilingual phrasing, locally recognizable references — not generic YouTube SEO advice.
- Every title must be checked against what the video actually contains. If a strong curiosity title oversells the content, either cut it from the list or tighten the phrasing rather than let it through.
