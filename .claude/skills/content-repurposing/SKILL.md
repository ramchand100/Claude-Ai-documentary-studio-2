# Content Repurposing Skill

Extract distribution content from a published episode — don't just re-summarize the whole video, mine it for standalone pieces that work on each platform's own terms.

## Process

1. Read the final script, fact-check report, and visual plan for the episode.
2. **YouTube Shorts ideas** — identify 3-5 self-contained moments (a striking stat, a strong quote, a 30-45 second sub-story) that work without the full episode's context. Note the source timestamp range for each.
3. **Instagram carousel posts** — 1-2 carousels breaking a key concept from the episode into 5-8 slides, visual-first, matching `CHANNEL_STYLE_GUIDE.md` tone.
4. **LinkedIn posts** — 1 post framing the episode's business/economic angle for a professional audience — more analytical tone than the Shorts, still no jargon without explanation.
5. **Twitter/X threads** — 1 thread (8-12 tweets) walking through the episode's spine and key facts, with the strongest hook as tweet 1.
6. **Newsletter summary** — a written summary of the episode for subscribers who won't watch the video, standalone-readable, ending with a link/CTA to watch.

## Output

`/Published Videos/EP###_slug-name/repurposed-content.md`.

## Notes

- Each piece should read as native to its platform, not a copy-pasted clip of the script. Tone shifts slightly per platform (LinkedIn more analytical, Instagram more visual/punchy) while staying inside the channel's overall voice.
- Pull facts only from the fact-checked script — don't introduce new claims during repurposing.
